## Context

What forces are at play? What constraint, discovery, or requirement made this decision necessary? State facts, not preferences. Cite the source of any external claim.

## Decision

The decision, in the active voice, as a single unambiguous statement. Include the specific values, versions, or mechanisms chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what new obligations this creates. Include the negative consequences — an ADR that lists only benefits is not being honest.

## Alternatives considered

Each rejected option, with the reason it was rejected. An alternative with no stated reason is not a considered alternative.
